# WordBound — A Vocabulary Roguelike

An immersive, habit-forming English vocabulary roguelike. Correct answers deal damage, wrong answers cost resolve, and every path offers a rich combination of battles, merchant bazaars, ancient shrines, anagram puzzles, and relic synergies.

Designed to turn English vocabulary acquisition into a deeply engaging, sustainable daily habit through intrinsic roguelike progression and spaced repetition science.

---

## 🎮 Play Locally

Open `index.html` directly in any modern browser, or run a local server:

```bash
python3 -m http.server 4173 --directory wordbound-roguelike
```

Then open `http://localhost:4173` in your browser. Fully supports PWA offline installation!

## 🌟 10-Iteration Evolution & Comprehensive Features

### 1. 📚 Vast Living Lexicon (180+ Curated Words)
- 180+ high-utility words across 6 thematic realms:
  - *The Verdant Verge* (Foundation & Nature)
  - *The Gilded Archive* (Academic & Analytical)
  - *The Iron Bastion* (Resilience & Strategy)
  - *The Whispering Mire* (Nuance & Mystery)
  - *The Sunken Citadel* (Deep Thought & Philosophy)
  - *The Astral Spire* (Transcendence & Wonder)
- Complete linguistic metadata: Part of Speech, CEFR level tags (`A2`–`C2`), Latin/Greek etymology roots, phonetics, in-context sentences, and Traditional Chinese (`繁體中文`) translations.
- Toggleable Bilingual Mode (EN + 中文) in Settings.

### 2. ⚔️ Dynamic Combat & Enemy Intents
- **Telegraphed Turn Intents**: Enemies telegraph upcoming actions (`Heavy Slam`, `Spark Siphon`, `Ink Leech`, `Fortify Shield`, `Strike`).
- **5 Enemy Archetype Traits**: `Armored` (shields), `Heavy Hitter` (crushing blows), `Siphoner` (steals sparks), `Ink Thief` (leeches ink), and `Swift`.
- **Dynamic Shield Mechanic**: Overcome defensive barriers before dealing direct HP damage.
- **Quick-Wit Speed Reflex Bonus**: Rapid answers under 3.8s grant +4 Bonus Damage, extra Ink, and dopamine particle bursts.

### 3. 🧙 4 Character Archetypes & 16 Synergistic Relics
- **The Scholar**: High starting resolve + *Scholar's Candle* (+20% rest healing).
- **The Bard**: Extra sparks + *Echo Chime* (+3 damage bonus).
- **The Duelist**: Aggressive quick-wit build + *Compass Needle* (+5 first turn damage).
- **The Cartographer**: Ink master + *Gilded Bookmark* (+30% ink gain).
- **16 Relics**: Including *Chronos Hourglass*, *Alchemist's Crucible*, *Oracle's Mirror*, *Phoenix Feather*, *Etymology Glass*, and *Resonance Horn*.

### 4. 🧩 Interactive Mini-Games & Wandering Merchant Bazaar
- ◈ **Lexicon Bazaar**: Spend accumulated ink to purchase rare relics, healing tonics, spark crystals, or insight scrolls.
- 🧩 **The Riddler's Chest**: Interactive letter-tile anagram unscramble mini-game.
- 🔮 **Word Alchemy Shrine**: Etymology root & prefix quiz granting permanent expedition blessings.

### 5. 🎵 Procedural Web Audio & Speech Engine
- Multi-oscillator harmonic chord synthesizer, ascending victory fanfares, level-up chimes, and combo arpeggios.
- Full pronunciation controls: Adjustable speech speed (0.65x, 0.85x, 1.0x) and Auto-Pronounce mode.

### 6. 🔥 Habit-Forming Daily Expeditions & 16 Badges
- **Daily Seeded Expedition**: Unique daily seed for consistent daily study habits.
- **7-Day Streak Calendar**: Visual habit tracker displaying consecutive active days.
- **16 Unlockable Achievements**: Badges celebrating vocabulary milestones, streaks, and puzzle masteries.

### 7. 🏛️ The Word Sanctuary Meta-Progression Tree
